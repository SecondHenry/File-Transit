Help

How to Send Files

  1. Click the + button or drag files into the upload area.
  2. Review your selected files. Use Add more files if needed.
  3. Choose to share via QR Code or Link.
  4. Click Send to generate a 6-digit share code.
  5. Share the code, QR code, or link with the recipient.

How to Receive Files

  1. Enter the 6-digit code in the Receive area.
  2. Click Download to retrieve the files.

History

View all your sent and received files in the History page. You can filter, rename, resend, download, or delete files.

Account

Create an account to keep track of your transfers and manage your file history. Without an account, transfers are temporary and expire after 10 minutes.

FAQ

What is the maximum file size?

You can send files up to 1 GB per transfer.

How long do files stay available?

Files expire after 10 minutes for anonymous users, or 48 hours for registered users.

Is my data secure?

All transfers are encrypted. Files are automatically deleted after expiration.
